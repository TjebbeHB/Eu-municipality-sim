Mayor's briefing

You run a mid-sized European municipality.

Every quarter, residents bring you passports to renew, bins to empty, permits to decide, care to arrange and potholes to fill. Process cases and keep people satisfied to earn points. Cases you cannot handle pile up in a queue outside the town hall.

Your budget comes from the national grant and local taxes. Spend it on staff, or invest in digital and sovereign technology: an EU Digital Identity wallet login, once-only data exchange, an open-source workplace, a sovereign cloud, a NIS2 security baseline, a governed AI assistant. Each investment cuts cost per case or raises capacity, which frees budget to reinvest.

  • Score grows with cases processed, weighted by satisfaction, plus a sovereignty bonus.
  • Cost per case is your efficiency index. Lower is better.
  • Risks are real: ransomware, licence price hikes, a passport peak, a storm. Some investments shield you.
  • The term lasts 5 years (20 quarters). Then you are graded.
